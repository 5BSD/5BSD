# Glossary of Names

This is the list every chapter of the book is checked against. Each entry
gives a one-sentence definition, the canonical name as it appears in the
tree at HEAD, the names it replaced (marked retired), and the chapter that
explains it. Retired names appear here and nowhere else in the book; a
chapter that uses one is wrong.

The rule behind the table: authority in 5BSD is a held capability, not a
uid, a PID or a path. Most terms below are the names of the things that
hold, mint, deliver or check such capabilities.

|---|---|---|---|
| 5BSD | A FreeBSD fork in which a mandatory kernel capability framework, a capability-native PID 1 and service manager, and sixteen brokered system services sit beside the stock BSD userland. | `BRAND=5BSD` in `sys/conf/newvers.sh`; `uname` still reports FreeBSD for ABI reasons | [What 5BSD Is](../orientation/what-5bsd-is.md) |
